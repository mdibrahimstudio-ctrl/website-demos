A neighbourhood café, roasted the slow way

Ember & Oak started in 2016 with a secondhand roaster and a simple idea: coffee tastes better when nobody is in a hurry. We buy from small farms we can name, roast in small batches, and bake our pastries fresh each morning.

Whether you are grabbing a flat white on the way to work or settling in with a book for the afternoon, there is always a warm seat waiting.
